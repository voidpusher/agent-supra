## Demo attachment and posting checks

- Use a reviewed demo showing a real agent attempt, test evidence, a loop flag or
  pause, and the dashboard. Label an edited replay **Edited replay of an internal
  test** in the video and post text. Do not imply it is a customer's run.
- Remove keys, emails, connection strings, account screens and private code.
- Do not claim universal loop detection, measured money saved, under-five-minute
  setup, zero bugs, or outside adoption. A hook pause blocks the next tool; it
  does not cancel an already-running tool or model request.
- Final video selection, the posting account and final text require owner review.
  Publishing and new outreach are not authorized by the existence of this draft.
